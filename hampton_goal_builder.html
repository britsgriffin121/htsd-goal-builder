    COMMITMENT
    Goal Commitment

    Write one clear goal sentence that names what your team will do, who will benefit, and why it matters. Aim for 15+ words. This is the anchor for everything else in this plan.

    Discuss first Imagine it is June 2027. What would students, staff, or families experience differently because this goal was accomplished?

    Format: Our design team will [action] in/by [scope or date] so that [student outcome]. Example: Our design team will implement student-led conferences in all K–2 classrooms by March 2027 so students can articulate their growth in Portrait of a Talbot competencies.
